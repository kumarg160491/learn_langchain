Under the sun, beneath the sky,
The cricket ball comes flying by.
The bat swings hard, the crowd cheers loud,
A moment of magic makes us proud.

The bowler runs with fire and pace,
The batsman stands and holds his place.
A spinning ball, a diving catch,
One single run can change the match.

The wickets fall, the scoreboard grows,
With every ball, excitement flows.
Through victory, defeat, and every fight,
Cricket keeps our hearts alight.

From dusty grounds to stadium light,
We play with hope, we play with might.
For every fan, young or old,
Cricket is a story waiting to unfold.
